# Goob Station Banning Policy

```admonish info
This is the ban policy for Goob Station. It applies only to our Goob Station servers. The admin policy can be found at [Admin Policy](https://docs.goobstation.com/en/community/admin/goob-station-admin-policy.html)
```

# Definitions
- **Indefinite:** Refers to a ban with no defined end time. This type of ban generally requires a successful appeal for it to be removed. Indefinite bans which have no extra requirements are sometimes called *appeal bans*.
- **Voucher:** Confirmation from the admin team of another well known SS14 or SS13 server that you have played on that server for a significant amount of time without any recent major issues. A voucher is required when appealing a *voucher ban*.
- **Voucher ban:** A ban which requires a *voucher* to appeal. These bans are often used as an alternative to a *permanent ban* that allows players to return if they can demonstrate an ability to follow a server's rules. See [Extended And Strict Voucher Bans](#extended-and-strict-voucher-bans) for details.
- **Warning:** A warning is a clear communication from an admin that some behavior is not acceptable. Warnings should always be paired with an account note making note of the warning and behavior.
- **Permanent ban:** A ban which cannot be appealed. These bans are sometimes called *perma bans*.
- **Game ban:** A ban from the servers. A player who is *game banned* cannot connect to the servers during the ban. These bans are sometimes called *server bans*.
- **Role ban:** A ban from a specific role or roles.
- **Ban evasion:** Attempting to circumvent an active ban.

## Special Banning Requirements

### Permanent Bans

Permanent bans should typically only be placed as the result of an unsuccessful appeal of an indefinite ban or a voucher ban. Except for cases of ban evasion after an accepted voucher ban, a vote by the admin team is required to place a permanent ban. Placing a permanent ban may be an option in a vote for the appeal. It is recommended that any indefinite ban after a prior voucher ban within 6 months of the date of the current ban be upgraded to a permanent ban.

### Extended And Strict Voucher Bans

**Voucher Bans** usually occur if a player is too problematic, or as a modification following an appeal. They require a voucher (proof of good behavior) from another server in order for their appeal to be accepted.

**Extended Voucher Bans** are applied if the player receives multiple offenses after an appeal ban, or if the player evades an ongoing ban. They are only appealable after 2 months and require a voucher **from a well known** Space Station 13 or 14 server.

**Strict Voucher Bans** are applied if the player receives multiple offenses after an Extended Voucher Ban, or if the player evades a ban a second time. They are only appealable after 6 months, with the timer resetting on each ban evade attempt, and require a voucher **from a well known** Space Station 13 or 14 server.

# Banning Guidelines

```admonish info
Administrators are not required to follow the times suggested by the banning guidelines when placing a ban, but bans placed within the guidelines are presumed to be of an appropriate length.
```

```admonish info
All instructions in the subsections of the *Banning Guidelines* section only refer to the guidelines, not policy. A statement saying "X is not permitted" means it is not permitted under the guidelines, not that it may never be done.
```

```admonish warning
Administrators who place bans outside of the guidelines are required to be able to justify the decision to the admin team.
```

```admonish info
Consulting the admin team via Discord, or consulting multiple (2 or more) other game admins in-game through admin chat or ahelp does not result in those bans being presumed to be of an appropriate length, but is sufficient justification for straying from the guidelines.
```

```admonish info
Any total suggested time greater than, not equal to, 7 days can be substituted with an indefinite ban and still be considered within guidelines.
```

## Evading AHelp

A player is considered to have evaded an ahelp if:
- They disconnect almost immediately after breaking a rule or after being arrested/killed for breaking a rule.
- They disconnect during an ahelp where they should have reasonably believed the administrator was investigating an issue that they contributed to or were suspected of contributing to.
- Without responding, they continue to play after receiving at least 2 reasonably spaced ahelp messages asking them a question since their last response.
- They do not respond at least 5 minutes after receiving at least 2 reasonably spaced ahelp messages asking them a question since their last response.

If a player evades an ahelp:
- Their ban should be extended by at least 7 days.
- Their ban may be extended up to indefinite.
- If their ban is extended, it must include an instruction to appeal to explain the situation or continue the ahelp.

## Ban reasons

In most cases, ban reasons should clearly communicate the reason for the ban to the banned player whenever they are not evading a ban. Admins should be able to easily identify the reason for a ban through either the ban or account's notes.

Ban reasons should follow this format for standard offenses:

```
Grouping Category | Offense | Sanction (W, RB, GB) | Offense Tier
Explanation
```

When multiple offenses are stacked, the following format is also valid:

```
Grouping Category, Grouping Category | Sanction
- Offense (Offense Tier)
- Offense (Offense Tier)
Explanation
```

Alternate account bans should typically be in the form `Alt of OriginalUsername` or `AltUsername alt of OriginalUsername`.

Bans of additional non-username information should typically include the GUID of the targeted player as a ban detail and should have a reason with just the account username. The earliest placed ban's reason will appear for the player when trying to connect.

## Dewhitelisting

Game admins may dewhitelist a player at their discretion in any case where an issue would likely have prevented whitelisting. Game admins are encouraged to pair bans which are either for a second offense of an issue or which are greater than 3 days with dewhitelisting.

[^abductorSabotage]: Includes intentional killing, sabotaging the station, or stealing unique items.

[^detrimentStation]: **Intentional** decisions by command that harm the station. Examples include ordering security to ignore active threats, using early launch without valid reason, or sabotaging their own department.



## Minor and Major Offenses

At admin discretion, an offense may be classified as **minor** or **major**.

- **Minor:** The offense is at the edge of rule-breaking and had no negative, or a positive, impact on other players or the round. It does not count as a full offense and does not accumulate toward the next tier for that specific offense.
- **Major:** The offense has had a significant impact on the round or other players, and the standard punishment is clearly insufficient. The admin may apply the punishment of the next tier directly, skipping the current one.

```admonish warning
Administrators who classify an offense as minor or major are required to be able to justify the decision to the admin team.
```

```admonish warning
Role bans and game bans run concurrently. To ensure a role ban takes effect after a game ban expires, the game ban duration must be added to the role ban duration when applying.
```

## Grouping and Stacking

- When separate offenses occur, the suggested time should be determined by summing the suggestions for each separate offense.
- Offenses across multiple rounds can always be treated as separate offenses, but are not required to be.
- Offenses within the same round are "grouped", and not separate offenses if there is no relevant ahelp between them[^ahelpDelimited] and if any of the following are true:
	- the offenses are non-grouping and one is necessary for the other to have occurred, or
	- the offenses are in the same grouping category.
- Grouped offenses should use the guideline for the most specific offense.

[^ahelpDelimited]: This means that if two otherwise grouped offenses occur within the same round, but the player is ahelped between the two offenses occurring about the first offense, the second offense does not need to be grouped with the first. The second offense may also be considered as a prior offense for determining suggested ban times.

## Examples

### AME Sabotage
A technical assistant sets the AME to 50. Their offenses are:
- Self-antag [Self-antag]
- Station sabotage [Self-antag]
- Unreasonable incompetence in Engineering [Engi Competence]

Self-antag and station sabotage share the Self-antag grouping category and occur within the same round, so they are grouped. Station sabotage is the most specific offense; self-antag is discarded. The two remaining offenses are in different grouping categories and are stacked:
- Station sabotage (first offense): W - 3d GB
- Unreasonable incompetence in Engineering (first offense): W - **4d** - 8d RB
- Total game ban: **W - 3d GB**
- Total role ban: W - **4d** - 8d RB

Since GB and RB run concurrently, the GB duration is added to the RB so it takes effect after the GB expires (W - **4d** - 8d + 3d = W - **7d** - 11d RB): **W - 3d GB + W - 7d - 11d RB**

The admin may also apply the following options:
- New player modifier (both offenses reduced to warning): **W**
- Role specific modifier on station sabotage (game ban converted to role ban at 2x, replaces game ban): (3d × 2 = 6d RB) + (W - **4d** - 8d RB) = W - **10d** - 14d RB

### RDM + Lying in Ahelp
A player kills a crew member without reason. When asked in ahelp, they claim to have been AFK and nowhere near the victim, despite logs proving otherwise. Their offenses are:
- 1x RDM [Escalation]

The lying in ahelp modifier applies (3x first, then +24h):
- RDM (first offense): **1d** - 3d GB
- After lying modifier: 1d × 3 + 24h = 4d; 3d × 3 + 24h = 10d → **4d - 10d GB**

### Security Officer With Prior Offense Categories
A security officer is killed during an arrest. While observing as a ghost, they identify where the crew member who overpowered them is hiding. After being revived, they use that information to track them down and beat them to death. They also made repeated errors processing prisoners throughout the shift. They have a prior IC in OOC [Metagaming] offense from two months ago. Their offenses are:
- Using info from ghost [Metagaming]
- Unreasonable incompetence in Security [Sec Competence]
- New Life Rule Violation [Metagaming]

Using info from ghost and the NLR violation share the Metagaming grouping category. Using info from ghost is the more specific offense; the NLR violation is discarded. The two remaining offenses are in different grouping categories and are stacked. The Command/Security modifier (2x) applies to Metagaming, but not to Sec Competence. The prior IC in OOC [Metagaming] makes the ghost info use a second offense:
- Using info from ghost (second offense): 12hr - 3d GB → C/S (×2): **24hr - 6d GB**
- Unreasonable incompetence in Security (first offense): W - **5d** - 9d RB

Total game ban: **24hr - 6d GB**
Total role ban: W - **5d** - 9d RB

Result (RB starts after GB): W - **5d** - 9d + 6d = **24hr - 6d GB + W - 11d - 15d RB**

### Station Sabotage With Casualties
A non-antagonist assistant, furious at being demoted, cuts the HV cable network in the engineering department. Two engineers are electrocuted and killed before power is restored. Their offenses are:
- Station sabotage [Self-antag]
- Over escalation or RDM resulting from station sabotage × 2 victims [Escalation]

These offenses are in different grouping categories and are stacked. The victim multiplier applies to the escalation offense:
- Station sabotage (first offense): W - 3d GB
- Station sabotage RDM × 2 (first offense): 12hr × 2 = 24hr GB
- Total: **1d - 4d GB**

### Metacommunications With Compounding Modifiers
Two players in an external voice call share a round — one is assigned as a Syndicate traitor, the other joins as a security officer and warns the traitor of active security patrol positions. When confronted in ahelp, the security officer claims to have had no outside contact with the other player, which evidence disproves. Their offenses are:

Traitor:
- Metacommunications [Non-grouping]: W - 7d GB

Security officer:
- Metacommunications [Non-grouping]: W - 7d GB
- Lying in ahelp modifier (3x then +24h): W × 3 = W + 24h = 24h; 7d × 3 + 24h = 22d → **24h - 22d GB**
- Command/Security modifier: does not apply to Non-grouping offenses

Traitor's resulting guideline (first offense): **W - 7d GB**
Security officer's resulting guideline (first offense): **24h - 22d GB**

### Grouping With Round Removal
A Captain, bearing a personal grudge, orders the HoS to arrest the Research Director without reason, strip their PDA and headset, and brig them indefinitely. The Captain's offenses are:
- Abuse of a position of authority [Conduct]
- Taking actions to the detriment of the station as command [Conduct]
- Unreasonable failure to follow space law [Conduct] — stripping a headset and PDA without valid charges violates space law
- Metagrudging [Metagaming]

The three Conduct offenses share the Conduct grouping category so they are grouped. The most specific offense is abuse of a position of authority; the other two are discarded. Metagrudging is in a different category and is stacked:
- Abuse of a position of authority (first offense): 3d - 7d RB
- Metagrudging (first offense): W - 12hr GB (×2, Captain is Command): W - 24hr GB

The Research Director is detained for the rest of the shift, permanently removing them from the round. The round removal modifier applies to the Conduct offense (2x): 3d × 2 = 6d; 7d × 2 = 14d → **6d - 14d RB**

Total game ban: **W - 24hr GB**
Total role ban: **6d - 14d RB**

### Extended to Strict Voucher Ban
A player under an Extended Voucher Ban evades the ban again using an alternate account. The alternate account receives a ban in the form `Alt of [OriginalUsername]`. Because this is a second evasion, the original account is upgraded from an Extended Voucher Ban to a Strict Voucher Ban. The player cannot appeal for 6 months, and this timer resets with each ban evade attempt.

### Warning Independence
An abductor kills a crew member for personal reasons rather than to conduct an abduction. They have a prior warning for friendly antag and a prior warning for round stalling from earlier rounds. Their offense is:
- 1x Abductor sabotage [Griefing]

Both priors resulted in warnings. Warnings do not count as prior offenses toward the next tier, so despite having two Griefing category priors, this is a first offense:
- Abductor sabotage (first offense): **W** - 12hr GB

### Warden Stacking With Prior Category Offense
A Warden re-arrests a crew member immediately after they have served their full sentence and strips their PDA without any valid legal basis. The Warden has a prior non-warning offense of unreasonable failure to follow space law from three months ago. Their offenses are:
- Unreasonable incompetence in Security [Sec Competence] — failing to correctly track sentence completion is a basic security competence failure
- Unreasonable failure to follow space law [Conduct] — stripping a PDA without valid charges is a direct space law violation

These offenses are in different grouping categories and are stacked. The prior is in the same Conduct grouping category as the current Conduct offense, making it a second offense. The Command/Security modifier does not apply to Sec Competence or Conduct offenses:
- Unreasonable incompetence in Security (first offense): W - **5d** - 9d RB
- Unreasonable failure to follow space law (second offense): 7d - 15d RB
- Total: **7d - 24d RB**

# Appeals

## Appeals Team

The Appeals Team is a group of admins within the Admin Team specifically tasked with handling ban appeals. Members are appointed and removed via Admin Team votes. Any member of the Appeals Team may handle an appeal on their own. Head game admins are always members of the Appeals Team.

An Appeals Team vote is required before placing a Voucher Ban as the outcome of an appeal. An absolute majority of its members must vote in favor for it to be applied. If this threshold is not met but the voucher option holds a relative majority, the ban shall be reduced to 6 months instead.

Any Appeals Team member may put an appeal to a vote within the Appeals Team or the Admin Team as a whole instead of handling it individually. The Admin Team is competent to decide on anything a singular member or the Appeals Team as a whole is competent to decide. A vote within the entire Admin Team is required where a Permanent Ban is considered.

## Appeals of Incorrect Bans

Unless the ban was an upgrade resulting from an unsuccessful appeal, if an appeal disputes the events used to justify the ban, the first appeal of a voucher or permanent ban may only be declined after it has been verified that the ban was appropriately placed.

## Appeal Hijacking

If an appeal is currently assigned to someone, it is generally best to let them finish processing it. Cases where it may be acceptable to hijack an appeal are:

- The processor has not responded to the appeal recently.
- The processor has indicated they are not going to process the appeal.
- A head game admin has authorized you to process the appeal.

## Processing by Non-Appeals Team Members

Game Admins and Trial Game Admins may not process appeals without the supervision of an Appeals Team member. Before an outcome can be made it must be approved by the supervising Appeals Team member. The only exception is when an admin reduces or removes their own ban.

## Appealing the Same Ban Multiple Times

After an appeal, a banned player with a correctly handled appeal is only entitled to appeal again if the ban is an Indefinite Ban or a Voucher Ban. The typical wait period between attempts is 1 months.

A player with a temporary ban may be told when they can appeal again by the processing admin. This is at the processing admin's discretion, but is typically 2 weeks or double the waiting period of the previous appeal.

A player may have their appeal reviewed or submit a new appeal regardless of any waiting period if their appeal was handled improperly (e.g. the handling Appeals Team member relied on incorrect facts). This may only be authorized by a head game admin following a successful complaint.

If a ban appeal is handled by the banning admin and the ban is not fully removed, the banned player may appeal again immediately.

## Appeal Procedure

1. Check for ban evasion. If found, deny the appeal and issue a extended or strict voucher ban.
2. If the ban has expired, you may, but are not required to, change the appeal into a [note appeal](#note-removal).
3. If you are not the banning admin and the appeal is extremely low effort, or a "troll appeal", deny the appeal. An appeal is considered to be low effort if it meets any of the following criteria:
   - The appeal is clearly the output of an LLM such as ChatGPT.
   - The appellant is clearly not expecting their appeal to be accepted, and made it simply to troll.
   - The appeal is incomprehensible or written in a language other than English.
   - The appeal has such little effort put into it that no other outcome can be derived from it but to close it.
   - The appeal is unable to be accepted for reasons outside the administrative team's control; for example, the appeal was clearly intended for a different fork or server host.
4. If you placed the ban, you are only allowed to process the appeal, but not vote on it.
5. Collect information:
   - Check the player's history of appeals, if they have relevant history post it in the appeal.
   - Make a reasonable attempt to verify any claims made in the appeal by the player, or accept them to be true.
   - Check the player's note and ban history to see if there is evidence of a pattern of behavior.
   - Read and link to the AHelp that led to the ban if it exists.
6. Run a vote:
   - Votes must run at least 3 days, if there is a tie or insufficient votes you may rerun a vote for the appeal.
   - Votes must be made in a thread for the appeal inside the designated Discord appeals discussion channel.
   - If an admin is to vote for "voucher" or "other" on an appeal, they should give sufficient explanation as to why they chose voucher and/or what they intend by choosing "other".
7. Post a response on the appeal which must be a summary of the following:
   - The outcome of the appeal.
   - If applicable, when the player is able to reappeal.
8. Mark the thread as solved using your response and approved or denied respectively, then lock the thread.
9. Implement the outcome.

## Appeal Format

**Title**

`Username - Ban Reason` (additionally add in if there is notable info on the ban, such as it being a Discord ban, Note Appeal, or a Community Approval)

**Text Body**

- Link to the ban appeal forums post.
- Banning Admin: Username or @ the user on Discord
- Additional Info: In here goes any additional information that may be important to the ban, such as it being out of policy, previously attempted to appeal, a noticed pattern of behavior, etc.
- Relevant AHelps: Link to the AHelps directly involved in the ban through the admin Discord. Not required, however highly encouraged so that we do not post AHelps publicly and so that the information and context is readily available and on hand.

Send the first message, then a vote for the unban or note appeal. Unban votes should include options for "Approve", "Deny", "Voucher", and "Other".

# Note Removal

Note removal requests follow the same procedure as the Appeal Procedure. Notes serve to describe a player's past behavior and help admins contextualize future interactions — they are not punishments in themselves.

When evaluating a note removal request, the standard for removal is not whether the behavior was minor or the player has since improved, but whether the note **does not faithfully describe what actually happened** or is **entirely out of context** (e.g. the note records an outcome that was later proven incorrect, or references an incident the player was not actually involved in).

A note that accurately reflects a real event, even a minor one, should generally be kept.

# Self Requested Bans

A player may request a ban for any reason, for any duration of time. Such bans are not considered a ban for further administrative actions. Self-requested bans may only be requested through AHelp or admin messages on the forum. Requests through other channels should be met with instructions to submit them through the proper channels. At the player's request, the ban may be lifted before the requested time via a ban appeal. While admins are not required to honour ban requests, they should be honoured unless there are compelling reasons against placing a ban.

## Self Requested Ban Appeal Procedure

Self-requested bans may be removed upon request by the player they were issued to unless the user has specifically requested to not be unbanned while requesting said ban. If this is the case, they may run a regular appeal for their self imposed "grass ban."
